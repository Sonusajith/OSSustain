info Empirical Interpretation Note

Decline probability represents the observational posterior likelihood of sustained monthly commit volume falling below 50% of the repository's 12-month rolling median during the 90 days following 2024-06-01. These metrics do not denote project defect rates or code correctness, nor should risk labels be construed as definitive indicators of abandonment. Causal attribution requires adjustment for historical feature confounders detailed in the formal pre-registration methodology report.
